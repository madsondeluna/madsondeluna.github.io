Bio Signal Hub

Eight browser tools for structural bioinformatics: PDB and FASTA conversion, sequence parameters, hydropathy profiles, contact maps, solvent accessible surface area, electrostatic surface potential and 3D structure viewing. Each tool opens in its own tab.

01

PDBViewer

Loads a PDB file, renders the structure in 3D and exports it to other formats.

 02

PDB2FASTA

Extracts the amino acid sequence from a PDB structure and writes it as FASTA.

 03

CSV2FASTA

Converts a CSV to FASTA, with the identifier and sequence columns chosen at load time.

 04

HydrophScale

Plots a Kyte-Doolittle hydropathy profile along a protein sequence.

 05

SeqParam

Computes molecular weight, isoelectric point and other physicochemical parameters from a protein sequence.

 06

ContactMapGen

Builds a residue contact map from a PDB file and plots the pairwise distance matrix.

 07

SASA

Beta

Computes solvent accessible surface area with the Shrake-Rupley algorithm, with probe radius and sampling density as parameters.

 08

ElectrostaticSurf

Beta

Runs PDB2PQR and APBS to compute electrostatic surface potential and maps it onto the 3D structure.

The hub is under active development. Suggestions and bug reports are welcome through the contact section of madsondeluna.com.
